Sean Isomatsu

Hello, and thanks for stopping by!
I'm a third-year bioengineering student at the University of California, Berkeley specializing at the intersection of mixed signal circuit design and biotechnology.

As part of Lin Lab and with the support from the Haas Scholars Program, I lead an undergraduate research team developing a wireless, steerable, and miniaturized ultrasound device for brain stimulation, blood flow rate/pressure monitoring, and imaging - under the mentorship of PhD student Nikita Lukhanin.

I am also a part of the student organization Neurotech@Berkeley where we design neuro-related devices, give and listen to talks, and share our passion for neurotechnology!

Previously, I worked on 3D-printed bone phantoms in the Lin lab, co-authoring an IEEE MEMS paper on pMUT ultrasound imaging.
